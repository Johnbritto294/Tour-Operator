We turn trips into stories

Founded in 2014 by three guides who were tired of rushed itineraries.

Fewer people, better days.

Stackly began with one van and a notebook of favourite family-run guesthouses. Today we run trips in 48 countries, but every itinerary is still built by someone who has walked it.

We cap groups at 12, pay local guides fairly and give part of every booking to community projects along the route.

What we believe

The rules we don't bend.

Growth has never changed how a Stackly trip feels. These four promises are written into every itinerary before a single hotel is booked.

  • Small groups. Twelve travellers, never more.
  • Fair pay. Local guides earn a living wage, not a tip.
  • Low impact. Offset flights and community-owned stays.
  • Real rest. Slow mornings, not 6am coach calls.
